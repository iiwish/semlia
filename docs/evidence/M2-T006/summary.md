## Delivered

- Migration `000009_m2_review_batches` (+`.down.sql`): `review_batches` (id TypeID `rvb_`, workspace FK, `grouping_rule jsonb` — the persisted grouping criteria, `policy_version`, status `open|confirmed|rejected`, `created_by`, `decided_by`, `decided_at`, `created_at`) and `review_batch_members` (PK `(review_batch_id, proposal_id)`, workspace FKs to batch and proposal, `added_reason jsonb` — the frozen creation-time decision snapshot, `decision`, `sample`, `split_out` + `split_reason`). Trigger discipline follows 000005/000008: `guard_review_batch_member` enforces that members are in_review snapshots of open batches (snapshot fields `proposal_id`/`added_reason` are immutable after assembly; a proposal cannot be an active member of two open batches), and `guard_review_batch_decision` enforces the one-way `open -> confirmed|rejected` step that must record reviewer and `decided_at` together, freezing decided batches. The downgrade drops only 000009 objects; `database_test.go` expectations moved to version 9 (inventory, tenant-FK list, step counts) without weakening any assertion.
- Expert review command (`POST /api/v1/workspaces/{workspaceId}/governance/proposals/{proposalId}/reviews`, body `{decision: approve|reject, reason}`): server-side `proposal.review` capability via the T001 evaluator (workspace-scope resource; allow+deny audited in `authorization_events`). Approve records an immutable `reviews` row (channel `expert`) and the proposal REMAINS in_review with no `decided_at` — approvals gate the T007 release cut. Reject records the review AND transitions in_review→rejected through the T002 state machine with `decided_at` (audit `governance.proposal.rejected` + outbox event). Unknown proposal → 404; non-in_review proposal → 409; second review by the same reviewer for the same channel/stage → 409 (documented immutability choice: one review per reviewer per stage while in_review, append-only distinct rows across reviewers). Response is the `rvw_` review record with reviewer `prn_`.
- Batch assembly (`POST .../governance/review-batches`, `GET` list with T003-style keyset, `GET .../review-batches/{batchId}`): one deterministic eligibility scan (`ListBatchEligibleProposals`: in_review + LATEST policy decision routing=batch + not an active member of an open batch) clustered by `(target_object_type, dominant diff category, matched rule id)` — the dominant category reduces the closed §8.3 flags by fixed precedence computation > access > contract > relations > definition. Each cluster becomes one open batch in one transaction: grouping rule, `policy_version` (the members' decision rule version), member snapshots with `added_reason` (`matchedRuleId/riskLevel/reasonCode/ruleVersion/inputsDigest`), and representative samples (first `SampleCount = 3` members by assembly sequence). Max-risk member is derived from the frozen added-reason risk ranks (ties → earliest member). The expert-routed proposal never joins a batch; a second assembly over unchanged state returns an empty page.
- Batch confirm (`POST .../review-batches/{batchId}/confirm`, body `{decision, reason}`): one transaction locks the batch and every member with its proposal, then per member re-checks the CURRENT state: the §8.2 decision step is re-run through the T005 `PolicyDecisionTrigger` before the transaction (idempotent) and the latest decision is re-read inside the lock. Members that left in_review, lost their decision, escalated to high risk (`risk_escalated:<level>:<code>`) or are no longer batch-routed (`routing_changed:<routing>`) are auto-split out (`split_out=true` + stable reason, review row withheld, proposal left individually in_review) — high-risk items are never mixed into batch operations (§8.4). The decision applies to the remaining members through the shared outcome rule: one immutable `reviews` row (channel `batch`, note = reason) per member, plus the rejected transition per member on reject. Samples are recomputed over the confirmed membership so the persisted samples always represent what was confirmed. The batch row freezes the §8.4 record: status, reviewer of record (`decided_by`), `decided_at` over the persisted grouping rule, membership, samples and exclusions with reasons; double confirm → 409; an all-split confirm → 422.
- SoD enforcement points (FR-007, server-side only): (1) expert review — the proposal author (`created_by` string compared to the acting principal TypeID) cannot review their own proposal → 403 `SEPARATION_OF_DUTY` naming conflict, scope (workspace), policy source and recovery actions, audited as an immutable `governance.review.denied` fact; (2) batch confirm — a reviewer who authored any active member refuses the whole confirmation naming the conflicting members (`conflictingMembers` in the denial details), nothing persists (batch stays open), recovery = re-create the batch without them or route them through the expert channel; the refusal is audited the same way.
- Shared review-recording helper (packet refactor step): `ReviewCommandDecision.Outcome()` is the single mapping approve→(immutable review, no transition) / reject→(immutable review, rejected transition with `decided_at`), applied unchanged by the expert path (`recordExpertReview`, which also owns the duplicate-review rule) and by the batch confirm member loop in the store.
- Authorization coverage: expert review, batch assembly, batch read/list and batch confirm all evaluate `proposal.review` through the T001 evaluator; every allow and deny lands in `authorization_events` (FR-013). Batch assembly is a review-preparation command, so `proposal.review` (not `asset.read`) guards it — documented choice per packet MUST DO 6.
- Audit record shape: the batch rows ARE the §8.4 record (grouping rule, member snapshots with added reasons, sample flags, exclusions with reasons, reviewer, policy version); each batch lifecycle step additionally writes an audit-only fact — `governance.review_batch.assembled|confirmed|rejected` (payload: `reviewBatchId`, `groupingRule`, `policyVersion`, `memberCount`) following the governed-object audit-only precedent; review denials write `governance.review.denied` (payload: `conflict`, `scope`, `policySource`, `recovery`, `proposals`).

## Review Boundary

Deviations and decisions for reviewer attention:

1. **`pkg/identity/id.go` is outside `allowed_files` but the packet mandates the `rvb_` TypeID.** Registering `ReviewBatch` (`rvb`) with the `ReviewBatchID` typed wrapper is the only way to satisfy MUST DO 1's "id TypeID `rvb_`" without a parallel identity implementation. Additive only; no existing prefix or test touched.
2. **`cmd/semlia/main.go` is outside `allowed_files`.** The production HTTP server wiring gained `WithDecisionRefresher(NewPolicyDecisionTrigger(...))` so batch confirm re-checks CURRENT policy decisions through the T005 trigger exactly as the tests do — the same wiring precedent T005 set for the worker.
3. **T002 latent bug fixed in `ProposalService.RecordReview`** (internal/**): it validated the review before minting its ID, while `Review.Validate` requires a non-zero ID — the method could never succeed and was never exercised until T006. The fix mints the ID and timestamp first, then validates and persists. T002's tests are neither edited nor weakened and pass unchanged; review rows are written with the same shape the T002 triggers and CHECKs enforce.
4. **`review_batches.decided_by` extends the packet's column list**: the §8.4 audit record must persist the reviewer of record, and the confirm path must freeze it atomically with the status/`decided_at` (CHECK: open ⟺ undecided). `added_reason` is `jsonb` (packet left the type open) so the full decision snapshot (`matchedRuleId/riskLevel/reasonCode/ruleVersion/inputsDigest`) is frozen per member.
5. **Review immutability choice (packet asked to choose and document)**: a second review by the same reviewer for the same proposal+channel while the decision stage is open is REJECTED (409 `CONFLICT`); distinct reviewers append distinct immutable rows (T007's two-person rule needs multiple approvers). The database trigger keeps every row update/delete-proof either way.
6. **Batch confirm SoD refusal is all-or-nothing** (keep-simple per packet): the FR-007 refusal rolls the whole transaction back — the batch stays open with no splits persisted and the conflicting members named in the denial (`conflictingMembers`); the operator recovers by re-creating the batch without them or routing them through the expert channel (recovery actions are part of the denial payload and exercised by the independent-reviewer test).
7. **Split policy reads "escalation" as any departure from batch eligibility**: `state_changed:<state>`, `decision_missing`, `risk_escalated:<level>:<code>` (high risk) and `routing_changed:<routing>` (e.g. medium/expert) — §8.4 forbids mixing high-risk items, and a member the rule table no longer routes to batch is equally not batch-confirmable. A confirm whose members are ALL split out returns 422 instead of confirming an empty batch.
8. **Samples are recomputed at confirm** over the confirmed membership (first 3 by assembly sequence; split members lose the sample flag), so the persisted samples always represent exactly the set the decision was applied to — the §8.4 "representative sampling" requirement read against auto-splits.
9. **SoD author comparison is `created_by == reviewer principal TypeID`**: it covers principal-authored proposals (including agent-attributed proposals whose run carries an owner principal, where T003 sets `created_by` to that principal). Free-text authorship (`createdBy: "founder"`) carries no principal identity to compare — an M2 authoring-model limitation, not a new one; the capability layer still gates every review.
10. **Batch lifecycle events are audit-only** (no outbox): `governance.review_batch.assembled|confirmed|rejected` follow the governed-object precedent, because no outbox publisher consumes a batch event type yet; proposal-level mutations inside confirm still emit the full audit+outbox `proposal.changed` events via the existing T002 path.
11. **Authorization resource coordinates**: review and batch commands evaluate `proposal.review` at workspace scope (the governance domain has no object-level scope; the seeded `reviewer` role binding is workspace-scoped), while proposal create/submit keep their existing asset-scoped evaluation.
12. **`POST /governance/review-batches` takes no request body** (assembly is a deterministic scan, not a filtered query); a non-empty body is a 400. The response is 200 with the created batches (possibly zero), since the call may legitimately create nothing.

Residual work belongs to T007 (release cuts consume the approvals recorded here; the author-cannot-be-only-reviewer two-person cut gate) and M4 (the auto lane, shadow evaluation, and richer batch re-clustering after partial splits).
