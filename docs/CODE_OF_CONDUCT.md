# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make the Semlia community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all. We respect the dignity, rights, and contributions of every participant, regardless of race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or social origin, socioeconomic position, level of education, or other status.

The same privileges of participation extend to everyone who participates in good faith and in accordance with this Covenant.

## Encouraged Behaviors

We agree to:

- Respect the purpose of the community and its ways of collaborating.
- Engage kindly, honestly, and with empathy.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Take responsibility for our actions and contributions.
- Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully.
- Repair harm when it occurs.
- Credit the sources of content and ideas we contribute.

## Restricted Behaviors

The following behaviors are violations of this Code of Conduct:

- Harassment, including violating an explicitly expressed boundary or continuing unwanted attention.
- Insulting, demeaning, threatening, or discriminatory comments or conduct.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Sharing another person's private information without permission.
- Impersonation or attempts to evade enforcement actions.
- Threats or encouragement of violence or other harm.
- Sustained disruption of community discussions or contribution workflows.
- Promotion or commercial solicitation outside the norms of the community.

## Reporting an Issue

Do not disclose a conduct report in a public Semlia issue, pull request, discussion, or chat. Use GitHub's private abuse-reporting channel and include the repository URL, relevant account names, dates, links, and a factual description. GitHub documents that channel at <https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse>.

Project moderators will acknowledge a report as soon as practical, prioritize safety and confidentiality, avoid conflicts of interest, and disclose information only to people who need it to investigate or respond. The project will establish a dedicated private community contact before opening its first public community forum.

## Addressing and Repairing Harm

Moderators may apply the following responses according to impact, context, and prior behavior:

1. A private clarification or warning with a request to repair harm.
2. Temporary limits on participation or contact.
3. Temporary suspension with explicit conditions for return.
4. Permanent removal from Semlia community spaces.

Lower steps may be skipped when necessary to protect the community. Retaliation against a reporter, witness, moderator, or affected person is itself a violation.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in Semlia project spaces and when someone officially represents the project in public or private spaces.
